# FarmTap Brand Guidelines

## The mark: Kraal Ring
Twelve fence posts around a kraal, with a tap at the centre. It stands for the farm (the kraal), the people and animals in it (the posts), and the moment of recording (the tap).

## Colours (Kraal swatch)
| Name | Light mode | Dark mode | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kraal Green | `#1F5E4A` | `#5FB89A` | Primary: buttons, links, active states, the posts |
| Kraal Gold | `#C98A0E` | `#F0B83A` | Secondary: highlights, the ring and centre |
| Cream / Night | `#F7F5EF` | `#0F1714` | Backgrounds |
| Ink / Mist | `#1B1F1C` | `#EDF3F0` | Text |
| Success | `#2E7D32` | `#7BD389` | Saved, paid, healthy |
| Warning | `#B26A00` | `#FFB74D` | Due soon, low stock |
| Danger | `#B3261E` | `#F2B8B5` | Problem, withdrawal period, overdue |

Gold is never used for text on cream (too little contrast). Use green or ink for text.

## Typography
Outfit (SIL Open Font License). Regular 400 for body, SemiBold 600 for labels and buttons, Bold 700 for headings. The wordmark is "Farm" in Regular + "Tap" in Bold.

## Logo versions
| Version | Words? | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Mark (`logo_mark_*`) | No | App icon content, small spaces, avatars, loading |
| App bar (`app_bar_logo_*`) | Yes, horizontal | App bar, website header, documents |
| Sign-in (`sign_in_logo_*`) | Yes, stacked | Sign-in, welcome, about, splash animation |
| Wordmark (`wordmark_*`) | Words only | Splash branding line, footers |
| White / black | Either | On photos or one-colour printing (cards, ear-tag labels, stamps) |

Use `_light` files on light backgrounds and `_dark` files on dark backgrounds.

## Clear space and minimum size
- Keep clear space around the logo equal to the height of one post-to-centre gap (about 1/4 of the mark's height).
- Mark: minimum 24 px on screen. Below 48 px use the small variant (8 posts, no ring); favicons and notification icons already do.
- Horizontal logo: minimum 24 px tall.

## Don'ts
- Don't change the colours outside this palette, stretch, rotate, outline or add shadows or gradients.
- Don't put the light logo on a dark background or the dark logo on a light background.
- Don't rebuild the wordmark with a different font.
