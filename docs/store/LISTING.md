## Full description (Play 4000 chars / Apple description)

FarmTap keeps your farm's records on your phone and shares them with your
managers, workers and the FarmTap website, so everyone sees the same farm.

WORKS WITHOUT INTERNET
Record everything offline. FarmTap saves on the phone and sends it when the
network comes back.

MILK AND ANIMALS
- Record milk for each animal and each milking in seconds
- Every animal with its tag, health, breeding and history
- Treatments with withdrawal days, so treated milk is never sold
- Vaccinations with reminders for the next dose
- Breeding, pregnancy checks and calving dates

WORKERS
- Staff ID cards with photo and QR code; tap a card to clock in
- Duty roster with stand-ins when someone is away
- Tasks, attendance and wages
- Workers sign in with their phone number and a PIN

FEEDS, MONEY AND REPORTS
- Feeds store: what is left, how long it lasts, what is running low
- Spending, sales and income, with reports you can share as PDF
- Daily farm report by WhatsApp

REMINDERS
Duties, vaccinations, calving and your plan, as phone notifications and as
free WhatsApp or SMS messages sent from your own phone.

MILK COLLECTION CENTRES
Receive milk by farmer card, test quality, close sessions and send farmers
their statements.

PLANS
Start with a free trial. Then pay for your farm with MTN Mobile Money,
Airtel Money, card or bank, and get a numbered receipt every time.

## In-app payments note (for reviewers)

FarmTap is a business service for farms. The plan is paid to Victorbee
Technologies through Mobile Money, card or bank and also covers the FarmTap
website. If Apple review asks for in-app purchase, the fallback already in
the app is: turn off "Pay inside the app" in the control panel (Admin >
Payments) for the iOS release so the screen shows "Pay with FarmTap support"
only (a "reader / business" style flow). Decide this at review time **(Victor)**.

## Review login (both stores ask)

Create a demo farm account for reviewers **(Victor)**, e.g.
`review@farmtap.app` with a password, owner of a farm with sample records,
and paste it in Play Console > App access and App Store Connect > App Review
Information. Do not use a real farmer's account.
